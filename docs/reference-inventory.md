# Reference site inventory

Source: https://sites.google.com/view/pupgs-mapsych/ (inspected 2026-10-29 in the built-in browser: live render of the home page plus raw HTML of all 28 pages).

**Ownership:** the site belongs to the **Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) Graduate School, Master of Arts in Psychology** (footer: "© 2022 PUP Graduate School"). Its people, documents, photos, history and location are PUP's, not UCN's. Use it for structure only. Do not copy its content into the UCN site.

Most of the content is images (slide graphics, portraits, photo carousels). Documents are Google Drive files embedded through `/preview` iframes, with `/view` links beside them.
